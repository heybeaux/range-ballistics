## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Pure solver module
The solver SHALL live in `src/solver/` and SHALL NOT import any DOM, three.js, or UI code. It SHALL
take SI inputs and return SI outputs. Unit conversion SHALL be a separate module.

#### Scenario: Solver runs without a browser
- **WHEN** the solver test suite runs under Node with no DOM
- **THEN** every solver test passes

#### Scenario: No UI imports
- **WHEN** the files in `src/solver/` are searched for imports of `three`, `document`, or `window`
- **THEN** there are no matches

### Requirement: Standard drag tables
The solver SHALL support the G1 and G7 standard drag functions as tables of drag coefficient
against Mach number, interpolated linearly. The tables SHALL be generated by script from a recorded
public source and SHALL NOT be typed by hand.

#### Scenario: Known table values
- **WHEN** the G1 table is read at Mach 2.00 and the G7 table is read at Mach 2.00
- **THEN** the values are 0.5934 and 0.2980

#### Scenario: Mach outside the table
- **WHEN** the Mach number is above the last table point or below the first
- **THEN** the solver uses the nearest end value and does not fail

### Requirement: Point-mass integration
The solver SHALL integrate the point-mass equations of motion with a fourth-order Runge-Kutta
method at a fixed timestep of 0.5 ms or smaller. Drag SHALL act along the bullet's velocity
relative to the air. Deceleration SHALL be `(π/8) · ρ · v² · Cd(M) / BC`, with BC converted from
lb/in² to kg/m².

#### Scenario: Vacuum check
- **WHEN** drag is disabled and a bullet is fired level at 800 m/s
- **THEN** the drop at 400 m matches `½·g·t²` within 0.1 mm

#### Scenario: Timestep convergence
- **WHEN** the same 1000-yard shot is solved at the default timestep and at half that timestep
- **THEN** the drop differs by less than 0.25 mm

#### Scenario: Agreement with an independent solver
- **WHEN** the reference cases in `tests/reference/` (made by Scout with py-ballisticcalc, G1 and G7,
  100 to 1000 yards) are solved
- **THEN** drop and wind drift each agree within 0.05 MIL or 1%, whichever is larger, and time of
  flight and velocity agree within 0.5%

### Requirement: Air density from atmosphere inputs
The solver SHALL compute air density from temperature, absolute (station) pressure, and relative
humidity. It SHALL compute the speed of sound from temperature. When the user gives altitude and no
pressure, it SHALL use the standard-atmosphere pressure for that altitude.

#### Scenario: Standard conditions
- **WHEN** temperature is 15 °C, pressure is 1013.25 hPa, and humidity is 0%
- **THEN** density is 1.225 kg/m³ within 0.001 and the speed of sound is 340.3 m/s within 0.2

#### Scenario: Altitude in place of pressure
- **WHEN** altitude is 1500 m and no pressure is given
- **THEN** the pressure used is 845.6 hPa within 1 hPa

#### Scenario: Humid air is less dense
- **WHEN** humidity changes from 0% to 100% at 30 °C and 1013.25 hPa
- **THEN** density falls

### Requirement: Wind from clock direction
The solver SHALL convert wind speed and clock direction to head and cross components. 12 o'clock is
a wind from the target toward the shooter. 3 o'clock is a wind from the shooter's right. Half-hour
values SHALL be accepted.

#### Scenario: Full-value wind from the right
- **WHEN** the wind is 10 mph from 3 o'clock
- **THEN** the bullet drifts left and the correction is reported as right

#### Scenario: Headwind has no drift
- **WHEN** the wind is 10 mph from 12 o'clock with spin drift off
- **THEN** wind drift is zero and drop is larger than with no wind

#### Scenario: Half-value wind
- **WHEN** the wind is 10 mph from 1:30
- **THEN** the cross component is 7.07 mph within 0.01

### Requirement: Zero-angle solve
The solver SHALL find the bore elevation that puts the bullet on the line of sight at the zero
distance, for the given sight height, on a level shot with no wind.

#### Scenario: Bullet is on the line of sight at the zero distance
- **WHEN** the zero distance is 100 yards and the sight height is 1.5 in
- **THEN** the bullet path at 100 yards is within 0.5 mm of the line of sight

#### Scenario: Muzzle starts below the sight
- **WHEN** range is zero
- **THEN** the bullet path is below the line of sight by the sight height

### Requirement: Inclined shots
The solver SHALL model uphill and downhill shots by inclining the line of sight while gravity stays
vertical. Drop SHALL be reported at right angles to the line of sight.

#### Scenario: Angled shot needs less elevation
- **WHEN** a 600-yard shot is solved at 0° and at 20° up
- **THEN** the elevation correction at 20° is smaller than at 0°

#### Scenario: Up and down are close
- **WHEN** the same shot is solved at 20° up and 20° down
- **THEN** the two elevation corrections differ by less than 0.1 MIL

### Requirement: Spin drift and stability
The solver SHALL estimate the gyroscopic stability factor with the Miller rule, corrected for
velocity, temperature, and pressure. It SHALL estimate spin drift with the Litz approximation
`1.25 · (Sg + 1.2) · tof^1.83` inches. Drift SHALL be to the right for right-hand twist and to the
left for left-hand twist. When bullet length is not known, the solver SHALL return no spin drift
and no stability factor, and SHALL say why.

#### Scenario: Spin drift present
- **WHEN** bullet length, twist, and twist direction are given
- **THEN** the result includes a stability factor and a spin drift that grows with time of flight

#### Scenario: Bullet length missing
- **WHEN** bullet length is blank
- **THEN** spin drift and stability are returned as "not available", and wind drift is still solved

#### Scenario: Stability warning
- **WHEN** the stability factor is below 1.5
- **THEN** the result carries a "marginal" flag, and below 1.0 an "unstable" flag

### Requirement: Optional Coriolis
The solver SHALL support Coriolis acceleration from latitude and direction of fire. It SHALL be off
by default.

#### Scenario: Default off
- **WHEN** no Coriolis option is given
- **THEN** the result is identical to a solve with Coriolis explicitly off

#### Scenario: Northern hemisphere drift
- **WHEN** Coriolis is on at 50° north for a 1000-yard shot
- **THEN** the horizontal impact moves to the right by a few centimetres

### Requirement: Derived outputs
For any range, the solver SHALL return drop, wind drift, spin drift, time of flight, velocity,
Mach number, and energy. It SHALL return the range where the bullet slows to Mach 1.2 (start of
transonic) and to Mach 1.0 (subsonic), or "none" within the solved distance.

#### Scenario: Transonic range reported
- **WHEN** a 168 gr load at 2500 ft/s is solved to 1200 yards at sea level
- **THEN** a Mach 1.2 range and a Mach 1.0 range are returned and the first is shorter

#### Scenario: Stays supersonic
- **WHEN** the same load is solved to 300 yards
- **THEN** both ranges are returned as "none"

### Requirement: Input validation
The solver SHALL reject inputs that are missing, not finite, or outside physical limits, and SHALL
name the field. It SHALL NOT return a trajectory made from a bad input.

#### Scenario: Missing BC
- **WHEN** the selected drag model has no BC value
- **THEN** the solver returns an error that names the BC field
