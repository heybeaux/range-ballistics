## Do not use (looks wrong on the maker's own page)

- Hornady FR320 (5.56 75 gr BTHP T2): page gives G1 .355, which is the 68 gr bullet's figure.
- PMC 223XM 16 in velocity (2420 against 2790 at 20 in): a 370 fps drop over 4 in.
- Hornady FR310/FR160 and FR200/FR100 show identical velocity tables for different stated barrels.
- Winchester figures come from the data service behind its pages, not the visible page; barrel NP.
- IMI: site unreachable. Federal XM855 and 5.56 69/77 gr: not found.

## Bullet diameter

SAAMI Z299.4, .223 Remington drawing: bullet ".2245 - .0030" in. SAAMI test barrel 24 in, 12 in
twist. SAAMI "5.56 Commercial" (2026-06-22) uses the same bullet diameter.
https://saami.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/SAAMI-Z299.4-CFR-2025-Centerfire-Rifle-Approved-2-10-2025-2026-10-21.pdf

## Barrel length and velocity

Maker-published pairs (PMC, same load at two barrel lengths): 5.56X 3270 at 20 in, 3120 at 16 in
(37.5 fps per inch); 5.56K 3100 / 2920 (45 fps per inch); 223A 3200 at 24 in, 2900 at 16 in
(37.5 fps per inch).

Not manufacturer data: Rifleshooter.com cut one .223 barrel (1:12 twist) from 26 to 16.5 in, five
shots per length. Stated average loss per inch: UMC 55 gr 22.5; Federal M193 25.7; Winchester M855
30.3; Black Hills 68 gr 22.8. The series is not monotonic.
https://rifleshooter.com/2014/04/223-remington5-56-nato-velocity-versus-barrel-length-a-man-his-chop-box-and-his-friends-rifle/

The two kinds of source disagree (22 to 30 against 37 to 45 fps per inch). Decision needed before
any estimate is shown.

## 5.56 ammunition in a .223 Remington chamber (SAAMI's wording)

SAAMI FAQ, https://saami.org/faqs/ :

> "However, it is not safe to shoot "5.56," "5.56 NATO," or "5.56x45mm" ammunition in a barrel
> marked as being chambered in 223 Remington for a number of reasons. The main reason being that a
> barrel marked as chambered in 223 Remington will have a shorter throat into the rifling than a
> "5.56" barrel which may cause increased pressure if the "5.56" ammunition is fired in it. This
> can result in serious injury or death to the user and/or bystanders, as well as damage to the
> firearm."

> "It is safe to shoot 223 Remington ammunition in a 5.56 military specification chamber. The
> firearm manufacturer can verify whether the chamber in your firearm meets that definition."
