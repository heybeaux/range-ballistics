Vortex sources only. The retailer page disagrees with Vortex on two values (eye relief, click
value); Vortex's figures are used.

## Reticle: measured from Vortex's drawing (NOT stated in text)

Vortex marks the drawing "for representation only". These layout values reproduce the labelled
values above at the same scale, but they are a measurement of a picture, not a specification.

- Centre: floating dot; the four arms start 0.1 from centre.
- Horizontal, 0 to 4 each side: ticks every 0.2 above the line; whole-MRAD hashes 0.5 above and
  0.5 below; half-MRAD ticks below the line. Numbers 1, 2, 3, 4.
- Horizontal, 4 to 8 each side: 0.1 scale centred on the line. Numbers 6, 8. Thick bar outside.
- Upper vertical: hashes every 0.5 to 4; 0.1 ticks added from 4 to 10. Numbers 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.
- Lower vertical: hashes every 0.5 down to 10; thick post from 11.
- Wind dots, one row per whole MRAD below centre, dots every 0.2 starting at 0.4 from the line,
  large dot on whole MRADs: rows 1-2 reach 2.0 each side; rows 3-4 reach 3.0; rows 5-6 reach 4.0;
  rows 7-8 reach 5.0; rows 9-10 reach 6.0. Row number printed at both ends.
