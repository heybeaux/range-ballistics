- Hornady ammo product pages never print a BC number ("highest-in-class BCs" copy only). BCs above
  come from Hornady's own BC listing at https://www.hornady.com/bc, which gives G1 and G7 at
  Mach 2.25 / 2.0 / 1.75 and says the Mach 2.25 value is the one comparable to other makers'
  published BCs. The 212 ELD-X and 225 ELD-M have separate 1:10 and 1:7 twist rows
  (212: 0.702/0.354 at 1:7; 225: 0.798/0.402 at 1:7).
- The 45 cal .452 250 gr FTX (#45201) and 395 gr Sub-X are NOT on the Hornady BC page, and the
  bullet product pages print no BC. No Hornady-published BC exists for the .450 BM loads.
- Remington and Federal (Power-Shok/Fusion pages) print a single BC with no drag model named.
  Federal Terminal Ascent and Gold Medal pages label G1 and G7 explicitly (GM3006M's primary BC
  field read unlabeled on our fetch — verify on re-read).
- Bullet length: Federal publishes it (GM3006M 1.220 in; P3006TA1 1.466 in; GM300WM 1.360 in;
  P300WTA1 1.622 in; P300PRCTA3 1.776 in; F450BMFS1 0.779 in; 450BMB 0.800 in). Hornady,
  Remington, Winchester, Berger: NOT PUBLISHED. Berger publishes minimum twist 1:10 and max COAL
  3.685 in for the 215 Hybrid Target instead.
- Test barrel: Hornady prints it on every table ("Test Barrel 24 in" / 20 in for .450 BM);
  Berger states 26 in and sea level. Federal, Remington, Winchester product pages: NOT PUBLISHED.
- Winchester product pages show ballistics only after JavaScript renders; the static HTML says
  "There is no ballistics data available at this time." Values above were read from the rendered
  Ballistics tab on winchester.com itself.
- Remington lists an AccuTip product recall notice, and a lot-specific recall for 300 Win Mag
  Core-Lokt Tipped (lots N16OCA373 / N17OAA373 / N22OCA373) — irrelevant to ballistics, noted only
  so nobody is surprised on re-read.

## Bullet diameter (SAAMI CFR drawings)

All from ANSI/SAAMI Z299.4-2025 (Centerfire Rifle), approved 2026-02-10:
https://saami.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/SAAMI-Z299.4-CFR-2025-Centerfire-Rifle-Approved-2-10-2025-2026-04-27.pdf

- 30-06 Springfield (drawing p.99, issued 05/29/1979, rev 05/19/2022): bullet ".309 - .003" in.
  Barrel: bore .300, groove .308, 4 grooves, twist 10.00 in R.H.
- 300 Winchester Magnum (drawing p.112, issued 04/21/1980, rev 05/31/2022): bullet ".309 - .003" in.
  Bore .300, groove .308, 6 grooves, twist 10.00 in R.H.
- 300 PRC (drawing p.106, issued 06/13/2018, rev 02/17/2022): bullet ".3084 - .0030" in.
  Bore .300, groove .308, 6 grooves, twist 8.50 in R.H.
- 450 Bushmaster (drawing p.156, issued 02/01/2008, rev 08/23/2022): bullet ".4525 - .0030" in —
  so SAAMI draws a .4525 in max bullet, consistent with the ".452" bullets makers load.
  Bore .442, groove .450, 6 grooves, twist 24.00 in R.H.
- Note the .30-cal oddity: SAAMI draws 30-06 and 300 WM bullets as .309 max / .306 min, while
  300 PRC is drawn .3084 max; all three shoot nominal .308 groove barrels. Use .308 in the app;
  cite the drawing for provenance only.

## Caveats

- Hornady trajectory tables never state zero or sight height; every table reads 0 at 200 yd and
  -1.5 in at the muzzle (except .450 BM at -2.4 in and the Subsonic at -1.5 in with 0 at 100 yd).
  Treat zero/sight height as inferred, not published.
- Federal/Remington "Average Range" and "Long Range" trajectory tables render the zero-point cell
  as an image, and the long-range zero distance is only "the range indicated" — both need
  human-eye re-reads before use as solver cross-checks.
- Winchester publishes no BC, test barrel, zero, or sight height anywhere on the product pages;
  their table lives behind JS (see note in Loads section).
- No maker publishes bullet length for any Hornady, Remington, Winchester, or Berger load here;
  only Federal does. No stability-factor math for the non-Federal loads until bullets are measured.
- .450 Bushmaster has no match/ELD-type factory load from any maker checked (Hornady, Winchester,
  Federal, Remington) — hunting and subsonic loads only.
- Berger table is the only one stated at sea level with a named test barrel (26 in) and stops at
  400 yd; its MV (2940 fps) is from a 26 in barrel vs Hornady's 24 in figures.
